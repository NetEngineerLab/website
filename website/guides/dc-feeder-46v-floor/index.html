Will 16 mm2 or 25 mm2 Keep a 60 m Feeder Above 46 V?

Example only, not a measured feeder. At 80 A and 45°C, 16 mm2 ends at 42.1388 V and 25 mm2 ends at 46.2288 V from a 53.5 V source. The 46 V floor in this sheet is an equipment input, not a universal telecom limit.

Battery runtime says how long the string can deliver energy. This example asks whether the feeder still delivers a usable voltage at the rack.

Example assumptions

  • Source 53.5 V. Current 80 A. One-way length 60 m. Conductor temperature 45°C. Go and return, so two conductors.
  • The calculator uses rho at 20°C of 1/58 ohm·mm2/m and a temperature coefficient of 0.00393. Example equipment floor: 46 V.

Steps

  1. 16 mm2: resistance 0.142015 ohm, drop 11.361207 V, 21.2359 percent. End voltage 42.1388 V, below 46 V.
  2. 25 mm2: resistance 0.09089 ohm, drop 7.271172 V, 13.591 percent. End voltage 46.2288 V, just above 46 V.

Boundary

Replace 46 V with the real equipment cutoff. The calculator does not add connection resistance, shared return paths or a fault current. A pass on 25 mm2 is not a cable-schedule approval.
