Will a 300 m OM4 Run Pass After Two Extra Patch Panels?

Example only. Three hundred metres of multimode at a 3.0 dB/km screen is 0.9 dB of fiber. With a 3 dB margin and an 8 dB class budget, two connector pairs at 0.5 dB still pass. Replacing that with four pairs at 0.75 dB drops the same run into a warning.

A 300 m multimode run is a different problem from the single-mode 10 km and 40 km cases. The fiber term is small until the patch fields grow.

Example assumptions

  • Distance 0.30 km. Attenuation 3.0 dB/km as a planning screen for 50/125 µm multimode near 850 nm, not a measured cable and not an IEEE reach certificate.
  • No splices. Engineering margin 3 dB. Class budget 8 dB from transmit -1 dBm and receive threshold -9 dBm.
  • Clean case: two mated pairs at 0.50 dB. Patch-panel case: four mated pairs at 0.75 dB.

Steps

  1. Fiber = 0.30 × 3.0 = 0.90 dB in both cases.
  2. Two pairs: connectors 1.00 dB, design loss 0.90 + 1.00 + 3 = 4.90 dB, remaining 8 − 4.90 = 3.10 dB. Estimated receive power -2.9 dBm, receive margin 6.1 dB. Status healthy.
  3. Four pairs at 0.75 dB: connectors 3.00 dB, design loss 6.90 dB, remaining 1.10 dB. Estimated receive power -4.9 dBm, receive margin 4.1 dB. Status warning because the budget remainder is under 3 dB.

Boundary

Passing the loss budget does not prove the modal bandwidth, the FEC setting or the transceiver reach. OM4 reach at 10G or 25G has to be taken from the optic datasheet. G.651.1 describes 50/125 µm characteristics; it is not a substitute for that datasheet. Do not copy the 8 dB class onto a different optic.

Sources: ITU-T G.651.1; The Fiber Optic Association, loss budget.
