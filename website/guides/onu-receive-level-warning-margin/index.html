A Measured ONU Level Is 1 dB Above Sensitivity. Is That Healthy?

Example only, not a live ONU alarm and not another connector-loss budget. A measured level of -26 dBm against an entered sensitivity of -27 dBm and overload of -8 dBm has 1 dB of sensitivity margin. A 3 dB warning setting marks it warning.

The three fiber-budget examples add connectors and fiber loss. This example starts from a measured receive level and classifies it. It does not design the loss.

Example assumptions

  • Mode: measured. Measured level -26 dBm. Sensitivity -27 dBm. Overload -8 dBm. No-signal floor -40 dBm. Warning margin 3 dB.
  • The -27 dBm and -8 dBm limits are example inputs. They are not a GPON class from ITU-T G.984.2 and must be replaced with the ONU datasheet.

Steps

  1. Sensitivity margin = -26 − (-27) = 1 dB.
  2. Overload margin = -8 − (-26) = 18 dB. The receiver window is 19 dB.
  3. The level is above sensitivity and below overload, but 1 dB is less than the 3 dB warning margin. Status is warning.

Boundary

A warning does not locate the fault. Confirm the reading with a calibrated power meter, then use the loss budget and OTDR evidence. ITU-T G.984.2 is the GPON physical-media recommendation; this page does not copy a class table from it.

Public source: https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.984.2/en.
