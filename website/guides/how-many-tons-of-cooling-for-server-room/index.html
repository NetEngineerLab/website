SERVER ROOM SIZING GUIDE

How Many Tons of Cooling Does a Server Room Need?

Cooling tons should come from a complete sensible heat calculation, not from floor area alone.

Sensible LoadPrecision CoolingN+1Cooling Tons

Quick answer

Convert the design sensible load to refrigeration tons using: Tons = kW ÷ 3.51685. A server room with a 159.25 kW design load needs about 45.3 tons of sensible cooling before equipment selection and redundancy checks.

Do not size from room area alone

Two rooms with the same floor area can have very different rack power. Start with IT demand, then add UPS/PDU losses released in the room, lighting, people, envelope gains and other sensible heat. Apply an explicit margin only after the heat sources are defined.

Worked conversion

For 120 kW of IT equipment plus 18.48 kW of other sensible heat, the base load is 138.48 kW. A 15% margin produces 159.25 kW.

Why 45.3 tons may not equal the purchased nameplate

The required 45.3 RT is a load, not automatically the final equipment rating. Manufacturer capacity changes with entering-air conditions, outdoor temperature, altitude, fan operation and humidity control. Match the load to usable sensible capacity, then add the required redundancy.

Frequently asked questions

How many kW are in one refrigeration ton?

One refrigeration ton equals approximately 3.51685 kW of cooling.

Can I use watts per square foot?

It can support early benchmarking, but rack and IT power data produce a much stronger server-room capacity basis.

Should redundant capacity be included in the tonnage?

Calculate the load first, select enough running capacity to carry it, then add redundant equipment and verify the failure case.
