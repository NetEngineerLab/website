Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling on the production NetEngineerLab website and its 20 bilingual engineering tools.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics 4 is configured on netengineerlab.com and loads only after you grant analytics consent. Rejecting analytics keeps Google analytics storage denied. Cloudflare may process limited request and performance data to deliver, protect and measure the website.

Local browser data

Calculation history and preferences are stored locally in your browser where a tool offers those features. NetEngineerLab does not receive the values you enter merely because you perform a calculation.

Manage your choice

You can change or withdraw your analytics choice at any time. This clears the saved choice and opens the consent dialog again.
