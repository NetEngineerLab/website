            Network architecture decisions within overall capacity planning

            Infrastructure capacity includes rack, space, power and network dependencies. Choose a network architecture by explicit requirements, team readiness and operating trade-offs before separately checking link capacity. Preference scores do not certify hardware capacity or redundancy.
