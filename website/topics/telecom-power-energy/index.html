Telecom Power & Energy

Plan site load, rectifier capacity, battery autonomy, generator fuel and solar support as connected parts of a telecom power system.

Start Here

Use the workflow below when planning or reviewing a telecom site power system. Begin with a measured DC load, then size conversion, storage and backup in sequence.

  1. Read the rectifier sizing guide to establish load, recharge and N-1 assumptions.
  2. Size rectifiers, then check battery autonomy and backup fuel.
  3. Validate equipment limits, battery age, generator operating rules and site measurements before approval.

Calculate and Plan

  • Telecom rectifier sizing — capacity, recharge and N-1 check.
  • -48 V battery runtime calculator — autonomy under the stated load.
  • Generator fuel consumption and runtime calculator — backup duration and fuel use.
  • Telecom solar and battery sizing — solar contribution and storage sizing.

Common Engineering Scenarios

  • Rectifier capacity with recharge and N-1 — size operating and failed-unit states.
  • One-tank generator runtime — relate load, fuel curve and required autonomy.
  • Site expansion — recalculate DC load, rectifier reserve, battery runtime and generator margin together.

Failure, Resilience and Operations

Check the loss of one rectifier, reduced battery capacity, generator derating and solar shortfall. A nominal capacity result does not prove backup performance during a fault or an aged-battery condition.

Engineering Resources

Use the linked guides and calculator methodology with equipment data sheets, battery discharge curves and site operating rules. Record the load boundary, units, ambient conditions and design margin with each result.

Recommended Learning Path

  1. Measure and define the DC load boundary.
  2. Size rectifiers and verify recharge/N-1.
  3. Calculate battery autonomy at the design load.
  4. Model generator fuel and solar contribution.
  5. Review fault states, derating and maintenance constraints.

Engineering Boundaries and FAQ

These browser tools do not replace manufacturer discharge curves, generator test data, temperature derating or an operational maintenance plan. Validate results against the installed equipment and site rules.

Why calculate rectifiers before batteries?

Rectifier reserve and recharge performance define the load that the battery system must support during normal and recovery states.

Does runtime equal guaranteed service time?

No. Runtime is an estimate from supplied load, battery and fuel assumptions; age, temperature, generator loading and faults can reduce it.
