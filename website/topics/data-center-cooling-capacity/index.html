Frequently asked questions

Can IT power be used directly as cooling capacity?

IT power is the main heat source, but in-boundary electrical losses and room gains still need to be added with a justified margin.

Why verify N+1?

A normal-state capacity pass does not prove the room remains controlled after one unit is unavailable.
