About NetEngineerLab

NetEngineerLab is built for telecom and network engineers. It provides tools for network planning, engineering calculation, design verification, and maintenance diagnosis. Values you enter are calculated in your browser and are not uploaded.

Experience and focus

The core team has 30 years of experience in the telecommunications industry, with long-term work in telecom engineering, telecom technology, and telecom network operations and management, including cross-discipline network analysis and field engineering practice.

This page does not list named individuals or claim degrees, professional certificates, or employer affiliations.

Practice areas

  • IP bearer, metro and enterprise networks
  • Optical communications, PON and ODN
  • Network planning, capacity and reliability
  • Telecom power, batteries, UPS and equipment rooms
  • Maintenance, fault analysis and risk review
  • Cloud, automation and engineering-tool development

How content is reviewed

Before a calculator or guide goes live, review covers the following responsibilities:

  • Domain review — engineering meaning and practice fit
  • Calculation review — formulas, units and worked checks
  • Standards review — citations only where a page names a standard
  • Product review — clarity of inputs, outputs and workflow
  • Technical review — implementation and automated tests
  • Release review — release checks before publication

Each tool is expected to state its assumptions, scope of use and result limits on the page itself.

Disclaimer

Results are for planning, comparison, design checks and fault analysis. They do not replace manufacturer parameters, field measurements, formal design deliverables or acceptance testing.

What is on the site

The tool directory currently lists 46 calculators. They are grouped by the work they support: optical links, PON planning, maintenance and OTDR checks, IP and switching, telecom and data-center power, wireless links, transport, and operations. Three calculator hubs collect related tools: PON and fiber, switching and bandwidth, and data-center power and cooling.

Longer write-ups live as guides. Topic hubs connect a question to the guides and calculators that answer it, including switch oversubscription, PON optics, data-center capacity, cooling capacity, telecom power, IP camera bandwidth and PoE, and network monitoring.

How the calculators are built

Each calculator explains the method, assumptions and limits for that page. Where a formula applies, the page shows that formula and its units. Where a tool page cites a standard, the citation is on that page — for example an RFC on an addressing tool or an ITU-T recommendation on an optical tool. A standard is not implied for a calculator that does not cite one.

Saved history, when a tool offers it, stays in local browser storage. Pages with an editorial check show a content-reviewed date, often in a structured “Content review” block.

Privacy

Necessary pages work without cookies. Optional analytics loads only after you accept it, and you can withdraw that choice from the privacy policy. Calculation inputs are not sent to NetEngineerLab just because you calculate.
