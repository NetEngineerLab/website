Engineering notes

    Calculation method

    Received signal level

    EIRP is calculated first, then path, additional and RX cable losses are subtracted and RX antenna gain is added.

    Pr = Pt − Ltx + Gtx − FSPL − Lextra + Grx − Lrx

    Fresnel clearance

    A common design target is at least 60% clearance of the first Fresnel zone; terrain, trees and buildings still require a site survey.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much fade margin is appropriate?

    At least 10 dB is a common baseline; critical or harsh-climate links often need 20 dB or more.

    Can this replace a site survey?

    No. Verify regulatory EIRP limits, terrain profile, rain fade, polarization, modulation rate and vendor specifications.
