# Telecom Rectifier & DC Power System Sizing — Engineering Method

Version: V1.0
Modified: 2026-09-10

Required current includes live DC load, battery recharge current and engineering headroom. Module count is rounded upward and redundancy modules are then added. Failure-state capacity and estimated AC input are calculated separately. Final design must verify manufacturer shelf/controller limits, module derating, charge policy, AC protection and local electrical requirements.
