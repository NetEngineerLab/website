Engineering notes

    Method and usage

    Constant-power load model

    Device power is adjusted for conversion efficiency, then a quadratic equation solves actual current and remote voltage—more realistic than a fixed-current estimate.

    Vremote = Vsource − I × Rloop; P = Vremote × I

    Pairs and temperature

    Four-pair power uses more parallel conductors than two-pair power. Copper resistance is corrected by 0.393%/°C. Verify cable certification, connectors and PoE class.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why does remote voltage change with power?

    Higher power draws more current, increasing both I×R voltage drop and I²R cable loss.

    Does this replace cable certification?

    No. It is for planning and review; field verification should cover wiring, resistance unbalance, contacts, temperature rise and negotiation.
