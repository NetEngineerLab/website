OOB Network Design Workshop

Plan conditional out-of-band transport, console access, management controls and power dependencies in eight steps.

Beta · Planning review only. No network discovery, production configuration or verified coverage.

1. Inventory

Count console-capable devices across the entire fleet. Counts are not per site.

2. Criticality

Targets are not verified coverage. Per-site inventory and failure tests are still required.

3. OOB Transport

Reported availability does not prove path diversity, cellular signal or carrier independence.

4. Console Access

One connection per listed console-capable device is an inventory assumption. Validate connectors, adapters and local allocation. No appliance size is inferred.

Conditional design review

Access-control review

    Assumptions and unresolved verification

    Fleet totals do not allocate ports to sites. One connection per listed console-capable device is assumed. Reported paths, power and user acknowledgments are unverified. Validate vendor constraints and per-site failure recovery before deployment.

    How to use this workshop

    Start with inventory, identify critical-site targets, compare reported transport candidates, review console and management access, then record security and power questions. The review combines conditional recommendations and trade-offs rather than selecting a certified topology.

    Worked example

    Three sites with fleet totals 10 routers, 20 switches, 3 firewalls and 2 other console devices imply 35 assumed inventory ports. A target of two critical sites remains NOT_VERIFIED: no site allocation or failure-test evidence was supplied.
