How the estimate is built

Base bitrate comes from the resolution and codec tier, then scales linearly with frame rate. Total bandwidth adds 20% for protocol and burst headroom. Raw NVR capacity uses Mbps × days × 0.0108 to get decimal gigabytes, then terabytes. Recommended usable capacity keeps another 15%. The uplink is twice the total bandwidth, rounded up to 1/10/25/40/100G.

The N-1 check treats one access switch or one uplink as failed. Use dual uplinks, a stack or MLAG, spread cameras over at least two independent paths, and make either remaining path able to carry the full video load. A single uplink is reported as not meeting N-1.
