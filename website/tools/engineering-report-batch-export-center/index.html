Frequently asked questions

Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. Parsing, combining and exports run locally in this browser page.

Does the center execute a generated MOP?

No. It compiles imported results into a reviewable document; engineers must validate and approve execution steps.

Traceability and engineering review

The bundle retains each imported tool name, source URL, timestamp, status, input and result. The MOP and checklist provide review structure; they do not invent device commands or approve engineering decisions. Validate source results and applicable standards before use.
