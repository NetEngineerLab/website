5. Rationale and migration review

Why not VXLAN?

If applications accept routed boundaries and no overlay segmentation is required, VXLAN introduces VTEP/VNI/MTU and control-plane work without a declared requirement. First document the missing capability, then justify the added layer. Preference scores alone cannot establish cost or benefit.

S-JUNIPER-EVPN

When simple L3 is enough

A routed fabric can be a candidate when applications tolerate subnet boundaries and no cross-rack L2 service is required. Verify addressing, ECMP behavior, failure paths and actual link capacity. Counts here do not validate any of those engineering properties.

S-RFC7938

When EVPN adds value

Required overlay segmentation or within-fabric L2 extension can justify evaluating EVPN/VXLAN. Validate service-to-VNI/VRF mapping, supported routes, MTU and multivendor behavior. A positive rating is not evidence that a particular platform meets the requirement.

S-JUNIPER-EVPN

When controller/fabric complexity is justified

Evaluate a controller fabric when consistent policy coordination and lifecycle governance are valuable and the team can operate the chosen model. ACI is one vendor-specific example. Compare licensing, APIs, recovery, ecosystem coupling and training before selecting; do not assume every controller fabric has identical behavior.

S-CISCO-ACI

Migration considerations

Migration difficulty remains UNASSESSED: the current topology, maintenance windows and application dependencies were not collected. Inventory gateways, addressing and policy; test coexistence, failure recovery and rollback. Plan a pilot with measurable acceptance criteria and operational ownership before expanding.

Sources and method

FitScore = sum of ten 0–4 ratings / 40 × 100. Every weight is 1. S-LOCAL-RUBRIC owns arbitrary editorial ratings; primary references support architectural principles only.

  • RFC 7938 — Use of BGP for Routing in Large-Scale Data Centers

    Sections 3–4 discuss traditional and Clos designs, and L3-only networks with L2 domains confined toward the edge. Informational operational experience, not a universal size threshold.

  • Juniper — EVPN with VXLAN Data Plane Encapsulation

    EVPN provides control-plane learning; VXLAN supplies an L2 overlay over an IP network. Overlay segmentation may serve multitenancy and required L2 connectivity; platform support remains vendor-specific.

  • Cisco Application Centric Infrastructure Design Guide

    ACI illustrates controller-coordinated policy and tenant/VRF/bridge-domain constructs. It is a vendor-specific example, not a requirement for every controller fabric. Validate controller lifecycle, policy dependencies and licensing separately.
