Method and guidance

Calculation logic

Recursive resolvers can retain the old answer until its TTL expires. Estimates add authoritative update delay and show the query-load effect of lowering TTL.

Worst case = max(Old TTL − Cache age, 0) + Authoritative update delay

Engineering note

Use the result for preliminary capacity planning. Verify equipment specifications, traffic peaks, redundancy policy and applicable standards before deployment.
