Limits

Limits

PMD-limited length = (allowed² − Σ component²) / PMD². Instantaneous DGD follows a Maxwellian distribution, so a max-to-mean ratio of about 3 is used for a design maximum. The 80% warning threshold is this tool's screening rule.

Methodology and engineering notes
DGD = √(PMD² × L + N × DGD_comp²); T = 1000 / R; DGD_allow = T × p / 100

METHODOLOGY

How to use

  1. Enter the PMD coefficient (the cable's PMDQ link design value) and the fiber length.
  2. Add the count and mean DGD of amplifiers, DCMs and other components.
  3. Enter the symbol rate and the allowed mean DGD as a percentage of the symbol period.
  4. Read mean DGD, margin and the PMD-limited length.

DGD formula

Fiber mean DGD = PMD coefficient × √L (ps), because PMD accumulates as a random walk. Independent sections add in quadrature: total = √(fiber² + Σ component²). The symbol period is 1000 / symbol rate in GBd (ps); the allowed mean DGD is that period × the chosen percentage.

Worked examples

Worked examples

Worked case: 80 km at 10.3125 GBd

0.20 ps/√km × √80 = 1.789 ps. Four components of 0.1 ps add 0.04 ps²: mean DGD 1.800 ps. The symbol period is 96.97 ps, so the 10% allowance is 9.70 ps and the margin is 7.897 ps (18.6% used). Design maximum ≈ 5.40 ps. The PMD-limited length is about 2,350 km.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

How do I calculate PMD for a fiber link?

Mean DGD = PMD coefficient × √length. 0.20 ps/√km over 80 km is 0.2 × 8.94 = 1.79 ps. Add component DGD in quadrature.

What is the PMD limit for 10G?

With the common rule of 10% of the bit period, 10.3125 GBd allows about 9.7 ps mean DGD. Older fiber with 0.5 ps/√km reaches that in about 376 km.

Why does PMD grow with the square root of length?

Birefringence changes randomly along the fiber, so the delay accumulates like a random walk, which scales with √L.

What is the difference between PMD and DGD?

DGD is the instantaneous delay between the two polarization modes. PMD is its mean value over wavelength and time, and the coefficient is that mean per √km.

Does PMD matter for coherent 100G or 400G?

Much less. Coherent DSPs compensate tens of ps of DGD, so legacy fiber that limits 10G NRZ can still carry coherent channels.
