Method and guidance

Calculation logic

Busy-hour demand applies average utilization, concurrency and burst allowance to access line rate. The result is compared with target usable uplink capacity.

Demand = Ports × Port Speed × Utilization × Concurrency × (1 + Allowance)

Engineering note

Use the result for preliminary capacity planning. Verify equipment specifications, traffic peaks, redundancy policy and applicable standards before deployment.
