About and applicability

This is the ratio of circle areas for the diameters you enter. It does not apply an electrical-code fill table, and a blank limit only reports the percentage.

This is an engineering approximation for planning. It is not a laboratory measurement and it does not guarantee equipment performance.

Content reviewed: 2026-11-19

How it is calculated

Fill = Σ(count × diameter²) / pathway_diameter².

The area of a circle is proportional to diameter squared, so the π/4 terms cancel. Fill percent is 100 × Σ(n_i d_i²) / D². Three 20 mm cables in a 100 mm pathway are 3 × 400 / 10000 = 12%. A second group left at zero count is ignored only when its count is zero; a positive count with a diameter is included. Set a limit, such as 40, only as your own planning assumption. The page does not copy code tables.

FAQ

Does 12% mean the code allows it?

No. It means the circles occupy 12% of the pathway circle.

What if a cable is larger than the pathway?

The fill can exceed 100%. That is a geometric comparison, not an installation method.

Why offer inches?

The inch input is converted by 25.4 mm before the same ratio.

Can I leave the limit empty?

Yes. The margin then stays blank.
