# Telecom AC/DC Power Capacity & Breaker Sizing — Engineering Method

Version: V1.0
Modified: 2026-09-10

The tool derives operating current from active load, voltage, phase, power factor and conversion efficiency. Engineering headroom and a user-entered combined derating factor produce a planning design current. The next common nominal breaker rating is shown only as a candidate. Final conductor and protective-device design requires applicable electrical-code and manufacturer verification including ampacity, installation derating, fault level, interrupting capacity, coordination/selectivity and voltage drop.
