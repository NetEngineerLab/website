Method and guidance

Capacity planning

Actual IT load is based on quantity and utilization, then growth and redundancy allowances are applied. Apparent power is adjusted by power factor.

Pdesign = Pdevice × Qty × Utilization × (1 + Growth) × (1 + Redundancy)

Heat and cooling

Nearly all IT electrical power becomes heat. The tool uses 1 W = 3.412 BTU/h and estimates cooling input from COP.
