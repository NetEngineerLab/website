Method and guidance

Calculation logic

Endpoints include users, their additional devices and infrastructure, followed by growth allowance. The smallest conventional IPv4 subnet that meets the per-VLAN target is selected.

Hosts = ceil((Users × (1 + Devices/User) + Infrastructure) × (1 + Growth))

Engineering note

Use the result for preliminary capacity planning. Verify equipment specifications, traffic peaks, redundancy policy and applicable standards before deployment.
