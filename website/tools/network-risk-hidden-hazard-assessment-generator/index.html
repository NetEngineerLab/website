    Method and boundaries

    For abnormal findings, the 0–100 planning score starts with likelihood × impact, then applies exposure, control effectiveness and assessment certainty. Normal items score zero; N/A items are excluded; unverified items stay unknown. Critical rules override the score when a named rule and visible trigger are both recorded.

    The output is a structured engineering screening record. It does not certify compliance with ISO, NIST, OSHA, electrical codes or local law. Validate applicability, evidence and corrective actions with qualified personnel.

    FAQ

    Does an unverified item count as safe?

    No. It remains unknown and prevents a safe conclusion.

    How is the risk score calculated?

    Likelihood and impact are adjusted by exposure, control effectiveness and assessment certainty on a 0–100 scale.

    What is a critical-rule override?

    A documented hard rule forces a critical result and exposes its rule ID and trigger.

    Can the report show remediation progress?

    Yes. Before and after states, scores, ownership, due date and review date are retained.

    Does the result prove compliance?

    No. It is an engineering screening record that requires local and professional review.
