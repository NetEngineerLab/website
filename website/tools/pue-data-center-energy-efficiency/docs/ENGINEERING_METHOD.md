# PUE & Data Center Energy Efficiency Analyzer

- PUE = Total Facility Energy / IT Equipment Energy.
- Power mode annualizes kW over 8760 h.
- Energy mode annualizes the supplied period using 365 / periodDays.
- Target-PUE saving holds IT energy constant and reduces facility overhead.
- WUE and CUE are optional ratios per IT kWh.
- Status is relative to the user-entered target, not a universal certification grade.
