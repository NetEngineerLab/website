    About & applicability

    Free planning calculator for data-center and facilities engineers. Runs entirely in the browser: turn instantaneous power or period energy into PUE, non-IT overhead, annual cost, target-PUE savings, and optional WUE/CUE. Application category: engineering utility / energy efficiency. No account required.

    Use for facility energy reviews, IT vs overhead splits, and target-PUE planning. Results are planning aids — not a substitute for metered boundaries, commissioning reports or enterprise energy standards.

    How it’s calculated · methods & formulas
    PUE = total facility energy ÷ IT equipment energy (same period)
    Non-IT overhead = total − IT
    Target facility energy ≈ IT × target PUE
    Planning saving ≈ measured total − target facility energy

    Keep the facility and IT meter boundaries identical across the reporting interval. Status colors are evaluated against your entered target PUE, not a universal league table. WUE/CUE appear only when annual water or carbon inputs are supplied.

    PUE definition

    PUE is total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy over the same period. Values closer to 1 mean lower facility overhead relative to IT.

    Overhead breakdown

    Cooling, UPS, lighting, distribution and other loads explain distance from the target PUE and feed the composition bars.

    Cost impact

    Tariff converts avoidable overhead energy into estimated operating cost and target-PUE savings.

    Boundaries

    Do not mix partial-month totals with full-period IT energy, or compare sites with different meter boundaries without documenting the change.

    WUE / CUE

    Optional annual water and carbon inputs produce WUE and CUE alongside PUE for the same IT energy base.

    Results are planning estimates. Validate meter tags, redundancy modes and climate assumptions before capital decisions.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What meter boundary should PUE use?

    Keep a consistent facility-total boundary and a consistent IT boundary for the same period, and document boundary changes before cross-period comparison.

    Can instantaneous power compute PUE?

    Yes for an operating snapshot; formal trends and annual comparisons should prefer integrated energy over a representative period.

    Why can PUE fall when IT load rises?

    If facility fixed overhead changes little while useful IT load rises, non-IT overhead is spread over more IT energy and PUE can improve.

    What does WUE add beyond PUE?

    WUE tracks operational water use per unit of IT energy so efficiency projects can be checked for water side-effects.

    Is a lower PUE always better?

    Lower facility overhead is usually helpful, but reliability, climate, redundancy, IT utilization and meter boundaries still matter.

    Official references · standards & boundaries
