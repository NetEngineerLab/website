    How to interpret the airflow plan

    The model first calculates the effective airflow that must pass through IT equipment from sensible heat and supply-to-return delta-T. It then back-calculates facility supply airflow from the entered bypass fraction, separating useful rack airflow from extra air required because of bypass.

    Recirculation and hot-spot risk

    The recirculation input estimates rack-inlet mixing between cold supply and hot exhaust. It is not CFD; it is a screening signal for row ends, rack tops, empty U-spaces, cable openings and other leakage paths that may need investigation.

    Airflow and sensible capacity must both pass

    CRAH/CRAC selection cannot be based on kW alone. The planner independently sizes active units by airflow and sensible capacity, takes the stricter requirement, then adds N+1 or N+2 redundancy.

    FAQ

    Why is cooling kW alone not enough to size CRAH/CRAC?

    A data center can become airflow-limited before it becomes sensible-capacity-limited. Adequate total kW does not prevent hot rack inlets when delivered airflow is insufficient or mixing is excessive.

    What is the difference between bypass and recirculation?

    Bypass is supply air that returns without passing through IT equipment. Recirculation is hot exhaust that reaches rack inlets again. Both reduce airflow effectiveness through different mechanisms.

    Does aisle containment always reduce required airflow?

    Containment does not change IT heat, but effective separation can reduce bypass and recirculation so less excess supply airflow is needed to compensate for mixing.

    Can this tool replace CFD?

    No. It is a first-order engineering screening and capacity-planning tool. High-density racks, complex raised floors, unusual supply paths and local obstructions should be validated with measurements, vendor tools or CFD.

    What does the N+1 check include?

    The planner checks both remaining airflow and remaining sensible capacity after one cooling unit is unavailable. Failure of either dimension fails the N+1 check.
