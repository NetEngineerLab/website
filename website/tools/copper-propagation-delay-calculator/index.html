How it is calculated

Delay = length / (velocity factor × c), with c = 299792458 m/s.

Propagation velocity is the velocity factor times the exact SI speed of light, 299792458 m/s. One-way delay is length divided by that velocity. Feet are converted with the international foot, 0.3048 m. A check case is 100 m at velocity factor 1: delay = 100 / 299792458 seconds. At 0.65 the velocity is 0.65 times the speed of light and the delay is longer by 1/0.65. The factor is an input, not a category rating.

FAQ

Does velocity factor 1 mean a real cable?

It is only the vacuum speed. Real copper is slower, so the factor is below 1.

Is the foot exact?

The page uses 0.3048 metre per international foot.

Why not use 5 ns per metre?

That would hide the velocity factor. The page keeps c and the factor separate.

Can this time a fiber route?

No. Use the fiber latency tool for optical group delay.
