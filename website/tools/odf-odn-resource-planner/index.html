Method and boundaries

The resource model converts the ONU target, effective split cap, protection factor and reserve into required capacity, then combines standard cable sizes upward. Worst-path loss is a design gate. The 3 dB warning threshold is a site planning rule, not a mandatory standard value.

The planning BOM excludes route survey, purchased-length allowance, vendor part numbers and pricing. ODF and splice capacities are user or vendor inputs.

Official references

ITU-T G.984.2 · ITU-T G.9807.1 · ITU-T G.652 · ITU-T G.657 · ITU-T G.671

FAQ

How are PON ports calculated?

The target ONU count is divided by the lower of the total split ratio and the operator limit, then rounded up.

How is fibre reserve applied?

Working cores are divided by one minus the reserve rate, rounded up, then assigned to one or more standard cable sizes.

Why can a plan be inconclusive?

Required topology, capacity, loss, reach or optical interface inputs are missing or invalid.

Does the BOM provide a purchase quantity?

No. It is a planning quantity and excludes surveyed route length, installation allowance, vendor part numbers and pricing.

Is the 3 dB warning threshold mandatory?

No. It is a NetEngineerLab planning threshold; project rules and equipment datasheets control the final decision.
