What this tool answers

Which supplied application is largest, what fraction of a link it consumes, and how much signed current headroom remains. Unlisted traffic is not inferred. Use mutually exclusive rows from one link/direction and accounting layer.

Worked example

A 1 Gbps link with Voice 250 Mbps and Backup 0.25 Gbps totals 500 Mbps: 50% current utilization and 500 Mbps headroom. Both applications tie at 25%. If individual peaks are 500 and 600 Mbps, their 110% envelope exceeds capacity but does not prove simultaneous overload.

Result interpretation

Current overload means the entered current sum exceeds the supplied capacity; inspect boundary, direction, time and duplicate accounting. It is different from configured port oversubscription. Missing average/peak values remain unavailable. An observed partial sum shows coverage, not a complete total.

Headroom explanation

F-HEADROOM: capacity minus summed current rates. Negative headroom is a deficit, never clamped to zero. Positive headroom does not guarantee loss-free service. F-PERCENT: rate ÷ capacity × 100. F-SUM sums only supplied rates; F-UNIT uses frozen decimal unit factors.

Common mistakes

Do not mix bits and bytes, ingress and egress, different sampling windows, payload and wire rates, or overlapping application classifiers. Individual peaks may occur at different times. A threshold is your policy, not a universal safe utilization level. Review an average or current value greater than its supplied peak.

Method and safety limits

Source IDs S-UNITS (existing decimal unit library), S-IDENTITIES (arithmetic definitions), S-USER-POLICY (operator threshold). R-COVERAGE preserves unknown values; R-PEAK identifies peak envelopes; R-THRESHOLD compares current utilization with your threshold. Safety limits: 100 rows, 120 characters/name, rates ≤1,000,000,000 Mbps, capacity ≥0.001 Mbps, 128 KiB snapshot; nonzero rates ≥0.000000001 Mbps and text numbers ≤15 significant digits. These protect browser resources, not network design limits.

Can I measure live traffic here?

No. Supply comparable observations from your monitoring system. No backend collector or network API is used.
