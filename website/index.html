Common questions

Can I stamp a design with this result?

No. Results support planning, comparison, review and fault analysis. They do not replace vendor data, a field test, a formal design or an acceptance test.

Are my inputs uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in the browser. Tools that keep a history store it on this machine. Optional analytics load only after you allow them.

Where is the formula?

On the tool page. When a page uses a formula, the formula and the units are written there. If a page does not cite a standard, it is not claiming one.

Which languages are available?

The site is in English, Simplified Chinese and Spanish. Some tools exist in all three. Use the language control at the top of the page.
