# NetEngineerLab V1.9.5-01 — Global Blank Section Hotfix

This hotfix overrides `content-visibility:auto` and oversized intrinsic placeholders in nine legacy tool stylesheets. Those optimizations could leave large blank blocks while off-screen sections were deferred by the browser.

Affected tools: fiber loss, optical power budget, PON splitter loss, ONU receive power, PON distance, OTDR event, MTU calculator, subnet calculator and bandwidth calculator. English and Chinese pages share the corrected styles and use the `1.9.5-01` cache key.

Calculator JavaScript, formulas, data, sitemap and SEO metadata are unchanged.
