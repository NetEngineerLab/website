## V1.8.3 scope

1. Validate form factor, aggregate rate, lane count and lane rate.
2. Validate optical versus copper/AOC media.
3. Validate SMF/MMF fiber type, wavelength and connector.
4. Calculate fiber, connector, splice and other passive losses.
5. Calculate worst-case receive power, overload headroom and sensitivity margin.
6. Return PASS, WARNING or FAIL with actionable engineering diagnostics.
7. Provide English and Simplified Chinese pages, FAQ, methodology and structured data.
8. Integrate tool catalog, homepage counts, sitemap, GA4, PWA and production checks.
9. Verify desktop and mobile layouts.

## Engineering boundary

The calculator validates physical and optical compatibility. Vendor coding, host
firmware allowlists and proprietary breakout requirements must still be checked
against the switch, router, NIC and transceiver vendor documentation.
